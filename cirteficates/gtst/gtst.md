Some courses teach you skills. Others change the direction of your journey. For me, this was one of them. 🔐

Before taking this course, I was trying to learn cybersecurity on my own through free resources, especially YouTube. I was motivated, but sometimes I struggled to find the right learning path, connect different concepts, and know what to focus on next.

Then my friend Yohannes Dawit recommended this course to me. I decided to take the opportunity and it became a turning point in my cybersecurity journey.

Over more than four months of learning, I explored different areas of cybersecurity, including:

🔹 Reconnaissance and Information Gathering
🔹 Network Scanning and Enumeration
🔹 System and Network Security
🔹 Web Application Security
🔹 Malware, Cryptography, and Social Engineering
🔹 Blue Team and Defensive Security
🔹 Wireless and Mobile Security
🔹 CTFs and Bug Bounty

But the most important part was not just what I learned. It was what those skills helped me do next.

At the time, I was a third-year IT student at Haramaya University, preparing for internship opportunities. I wanted to gain practical experience in cybersecurity and take my learning beyond theory.

This course helped me move closer to that goal. Thanks to Geez Security

✅ It helped me secure a cybersecurity internship opportunity at Ipcom Technologies

✅ It strengthened my preparation for the INSA Summer Camp, organized by the Information Network Security Administration (INSA) and held at Adama Science and Technology University (ASTU), where I successfully get more knowledge on cybersecurity. 

✅ It gave me the foundation and confidence to continue exploring cybersecurity through hands-on practice, CTFs, and independent labs.

Looking back, I’m grateful that I started, grateful to Yohannes Dawit for recommending the course, and grateful for the knowledge and opportunities that followed.

Special thanks to Natan Hailu and Nehimya Mesfin for being part of this important chapter of my cybersecurity journey. Your teaching and guidance helped make this experience valuable and memorable.

My journey is far from over. I’m continuing to develop my skills, explore new challenges, and grow in both offensive and defensive security, with a particular interest in practical security testing and threat detection.

To anyone learning cybersecurity through free resources and feeling lost sometimes: keep going. Find a learning path, practice what you study, and don't be afraid to ask for guidance. One opportunity can make a real difference and i recommend you this course.
